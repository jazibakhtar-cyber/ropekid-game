RopeKid Privacy Policy

RopeKid is a mobile puzzle game developed by Moon Games. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information and Local Game Data

RopeKid does not require an account and does not ask players to enter personal information. Game progress, coins, unlocked puzzles, purchased characters, backgrounds, and settings may be stored locally on the device. Deleting the app or clearing its data may remove this progress.

Advertising

RopeKid may display advertising provided by Google AdMob. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. How Google uses this information is explained in Google's partner sites policy. Where required, players will be offered applicable privacy choices or consent controls.

Permissions

RopeKid does not request access to sensitive device features such as the camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, SMS, or phone calls.

Children's Privacy

RopeKid does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. Parents or guardians who believe a child has supplied personal information may contact Moon Games.

Data Sharing and Security

Moon Games does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers such as Google only to provide their services, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled through the game.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game's features or services change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
